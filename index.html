Why this product split works

The Lite app is the top of the funnel — high-volume free downloads via the App Store generate ad revenue, the $1.99 remove-ads IAP converts about 2-3% of active users, and every install becomes a warm lead for Pro.

  1. Discover — A coach or parent searches "volleyball scoreboard" in the App Store and downloads Lite.
  2. Use & trust — They use it for 3-4 matches. Become familiar with the brand and the UX.
  3. Aspire — They see the "Go Live with Pro · Stream this match to fans" upgrade banner. Curiosity builds.
  4. Convert — When their kid plays a tournament far from home, they upgrade to Pro to share with family.
  5. Stick — Auto-clips of their child make it impossible to cancel after 2 months.

Roadmap · next 90 days

Month 1

Lite to App Store

Wrap the HTML in Capacitor or React Native, register Apple ($99/yr) and Google Play ($25 once), launch with banner ads + IAP. Goal: 1,000 downloads.

Firebase or Supabase for real-time score sync. AWS IVS or Mux for live RTMP. Closed beta with 3-5 pilot clubs. Web/PWA first, no App Store yet.
